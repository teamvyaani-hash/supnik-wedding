02

MINIMUM VIABLE COMMUNICATION

Speak something.

Four phrases from each side. Enough to demonstrate effort. Not enough to start giving speeches.

Tamil

For the Bangalore delegation entering Chennai territory.

Vanakkam Hello / Greetings

Start here. Confidence can arrive later.

Nandri Thank you

Surprisingly useful at weddings.

Semma! Amazing / Excellent

Suitable for outfits, music and dramatic entrances.

Konjam adjust pannunga Please adjust a little

A phrase with suspiciously pan-Indian usefulness.

Kannada

For the Chennai delegation attempting Bangalore diplomacy.

Namaskara Hello / Greetings

Say it confidently and you’re already doing well.

Dhanyavadagalu Thank you

Difficult spelling. Excellent intentions.

Sakkath! Awesome / Excellent

Deploy generously.

Swalpa adjust maadi Please adjust a little

Bangalore's unofficial constitution.
