CITY DIPLOMACY

A few things we should settle.

We are bringing Bangalore and Chennai together for an entire weekend. Some ground rules are therefore necessary.

01

“I'm five minutes away.”

In Bangalore, this is a philosophical statement and should not be interpreted as a measurement of time.

02

Chennai weather.

You may complain about the heat. You may then immediately drink hot filter coffee. This is normal.

03

Directions.

“After the temple, before that bakery, take the road near that one signal” remains a valid navigation system.

04

“Small function.”

Could mean 40 people. Could mean 600. No further information will be provided.
